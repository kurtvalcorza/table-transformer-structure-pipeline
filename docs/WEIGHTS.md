# Weight provenance and DIMER hosting

- Upstream: `microsoft/table-transformer-structure-recognition-v1.1-all`
- Immutable revision: `7587a7ef111d9dcbf8ac695f1376ab7014340a0c`
- Weight format: SafeTensors (`model.safetensors`, 115,437,156 bytes); the upstream repository hosts no pickle checkpoint at this revision.
- Manifest: `weights/table-transformer-structure-v1.1-all/dimer-base-manifest.json` (4 files: `README.md`, `config.json`, `model.safetensors`, `preprocessor_config.json`; 115,515,347 bytes total, per-file SHA-256)
- Upstream weight license: MIT (the checkpoint's `README.md` front matter)
- DIMER hosting: MIT permits use, modification, distribution, and commercial use subject to preservation of the copyright notice and licence text. The Git repository does not vendor the checkpoint (`weights/**/*.safetensors` is git-ignored); DIMER may mirror the pinned snapshot in its model store under the upstream license.
- Fresh clone: `stage_missing_files(allow_download=True)` fetches only the manifest-listed files absent on disk, at the pinned revision, into the snapshot directory; `verify_snapshot()` then checks every file before any load.
- Loader trust boundary: Transformers `TableTransformerForObjectDetection` / `AutoImageProcessor` with `trust_remote_code=False`, `local_files_only=True` from the verified directory; the backbone is the in-library ResNet described by the config's `backbone_config` (`use_timm_backbone: false`), so nothing is fetched at construction (the smoke run loaded with `HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1`). The image-processor `size` is passed as `{"shortest_edge": 800, "longest_edge": 800}` because the pinned `preprocessor_config.json` form (`{"longest_edge": 800}`) is refused by the pinned transformers release; the two are the same resize.

## Adaptation corpus (tutorial data, not weights)

- Corpus: 94 scientific tables from SciTSR-PD, the public-domain subset of SciTSR (Chi et al. 2019; arXiv LaTeX tables rendered at 150 DPI) whose source papers carry a CC0 or the older Creative Commons public-domain dedication, as published on the Hugging Face Hub as `bevaya/SciTSR-pd` (licence tag cc0-1.0) at commit `dae336ef` (the full 40-hex commit is recorded in `sample_data.py`) — two parquet files pinned by size and SHA-256 in `sample_data.SOURCE_FILES` (`data/train-00000-of-00001.parquet`, 5,185,141 bytes, `d295b918…`; `data/test-00000-of-00001.parquet`, 1,066,042 bytes, `73af6317…`). The PNGs are the dataset's own renders (mode L), re-encoded losslessly (pixels asserted identical) and embedded as base64 in `src/table_transformer_structure_pipeline/sample_data.py` (1.3 MB source, 0.9 MB of PNG) so the standalone notebook needs no parquet reader or download; each carries its recorded byte size and SHA-256 and is refused on any mismatch. SciTSR's own train / test split shares papers, so it is not used: the sample is split by paper with a seeded draw (10 / 7 / 29 papers → 21 / 23 / 50 tables).
- Derivation (once, 2026-09-19, by `scitsr_align.py` / `scitsr_derive.py` / `scitsr_build_sample_data.py`, recorded in the build ledger): (1) the dataset's text chunks (PDF points, bottom-left origin) are placed on the trimmed image by a whole-pixel offset search at scale 150/72 that maximises ink coverage inside the chunk boxes, constrained so every chunk stays inside the image (`alignment` records the offset — 16 / 11 px for most tables — and the coverage score); (2) chunks are matched to the logical cells by normalised text (exact matches first, then repeated texts zipped in reading order, then multi-chunk continuations; the conversion's doubled final character of every table's last chunk is undone); (3) the `table` box is the ink bounding box around the cell union expanded by 1.5 row / column pitches (rules included, captions excluded), `table row` / `table column` boxes tile it with boundaries at the mid-gaps between adjacent rows / columns (the PubTables-1M convention), and every cell spanning more than one row or column becomes a `table spanning cell` over its grid area. Tables with an unmatched text cell, overlapping rows or columns after matching, or a row or column without a single-span text cell were dropped: 14 of 108. Column headers and projected row headers are not annotated in SciTSR and are absent.
- Adapter artifacts written by the tutorial (`outputs/table_transformer_structure_adapter/`, `org.valcorza.table-transformer-structure.adapter.v1`, about 0.5 MB for the heads alone, 13.2 MB with two trained decoder layers) carry the restricted class head, the trained copy of the box head and any trained decoder-layer tensors, with a manifest naming the base `model.safetensors` digest, the classes and the policy; the checkpoint's own heads are never part of them; they are outputs, not hosted weights. DIMER hosting of the weights is unaffected.
